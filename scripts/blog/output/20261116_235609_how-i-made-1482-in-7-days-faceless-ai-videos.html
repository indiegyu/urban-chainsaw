Step 3: Generating Cinematic Visuals and Assembling the Video

With a killer script and a studio-quality voiceover ready to go, I needed striking visuals to match. Faceless videos live and die by their B-roll footage. Instead of hunting through copyright-free stock video libraries for hours, I turned to AI image and video generators. After gathering my clips, the editing phase was surprisingly straightforward. I dropped the AI voiceover into a timeline, arranged the generated visuals to sync with the audio beats, and added dynamic captions. Dynamic captions are an absolute must for modern video platforms because a huge percentage of viewers watch content with the sound off. Keeping your text engaging, colorful, and fast-paced ensures retention stays high. Within less than an hour per video, I had a polished, professional-grade piece of content ready for upload, completely bypassing the steep learning curve of traditional, complex editing software.
