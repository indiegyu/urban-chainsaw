I focused on the personal finance and luxury lifestyle intersection, which traditionally attracts high advertiser bids. Before writing a single script, I spent days analyzing competitor channels using free browser extensions. I looked for gaps in their content, high-performing outlier videos, and topics that consistently trended. Crucially, I avoided hyper-saturated spaces where human-hosted channels dominate with personal charisma. Instead, I targeted curiosity-driven topics like historical mysteries, billionaire biographies, and futuristic technology. This strategic positioning ensured that viewers stayed engaged purely based on the quality of the storytelling and visuals, entirely bypassing the need for a host. By treating niche research as a data-driven science rather than a guessing game, I set the stage for explosive organic growth from day one.
